Help
  1. Connect to the lead laser (cable in DMX OUT). The other laser in slave mode copies it.
  2. Both lasers share one Bluetooth ID, so only one can connect. That's normal.
  3. After a power-off the laser goes back to its back-panel mode. Tap a show again.
  4. No laser in the list? Close LightElf, check phone Bluetooth is on, switch the laser off and on.
