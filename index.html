Help
  1. Connect to the lead laser (cable in DMX OUT). The other laser in slave mode copies it.
  2. Both lasers share one Bluetooth ID, so only one can connect. That's normal.
  3. One laser animations, the other beams: unplug the DMX cable and set the second laser to sound mode on its back panel (SA). Connect to the first laser and tap ANIMATIONS or RAVE + ANIMATIONS.
  4. After a power-off the laser goes back to its back-panel mode. Tap a show again.
  5. No laser in the list? Close LightElf, check phone Bluetooth is on, switch the laser off and on.
