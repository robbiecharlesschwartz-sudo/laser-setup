Setup notes
  1. Connect to the lead laser (cable in DMX OUT). The follower, in slave mode, copies it.
  2. Only one app can hold a laser at a time — close LightElf and other tabs first.
  3. After a power-off the laser returns to its back-panel mode. Tap a show again.
  4. Not listed? Turn phone Bluetooth on, power-cycle the laser, try again.
  5. Rave and Rave + Anim run on the lasers' own microphone: they need the music loud near the lasers (phone speakers are too quiet). Sensitivity is in Fine-tune.
  6. Aim and Spin work in every show: in Club they steer the running show; in the others they take over with an aimable beam while you hold, then hand the show back.
  7. Blackout switches the lasers to standby (fully off). Tap any show to turn them back on.
  8. Party Set is a smooth sequence the lasers play by themselves; it changes every minute.
  9. Mount the lasers above head height. Never aim beams at people.
