Laser Setup

Puts LightElf (TD5322A) lasers into sound mode. Settings stay on the laser.

This browser can't use Bluetooth.
  1. iPhone/iPad: open this page in the free Bluefy app (Safari can't do Bluetooth).
  2. Mac/PC/Android: use Chrome or Edge.
  1. Close LightElf on every phone (it holds the laser).
  2. Both lasers share one Bluetooth ID, so do one at a time: only one laser powered on.
  3. Connect, pick settings, tap Save to laser. Then swap lasers and repeat.

RAVE, Laser lines and All patterns use SOUND mode, so the lasers change on the beat. RAVE AUTO keeps moving by itself at a set speed. Rave and Laser lines play only straight lines and dots: no shapes, no animations. Best in haze.
